## Step 1: Understanding the Shock

This is a **dovish surprise** — the central bank hiked less than expected. Relative to market pricing:
- The actual hike (25bp) is tighter than the prior stance
- But it's *looser* than what markets had priced in (50bp)

The net effect relative to expectations is a **25bp dovish surprise** (easing relative to expectations), even though in absolute terms policy tightened 25bp.

- VTR **depreciates** relative to where it would have been (markets expected more tightening)
