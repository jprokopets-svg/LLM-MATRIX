# Macroeconomic Forecasting Analysis: Republic of Vantria

## Step-by-Step Causal Reasoning

### 1. Direct Effects of Supply Chain Shock

**Inflation Impact:**
- Current inflation (Q60): 1.4% year-on-year
- Supply chain easing shock: -0.75 percentage points
- Direct mechanical effect on inflation: 1.4% - 0.75% = **0.65%** at 1 quarter ahead

This is a favorable supply-side shock that reduces input costs and import prices directly.

**Real Interest Rate Impact:**
- Current nominal policy rate (Q60): 1.7%
- Current inflation (Q60): 1.4%
- Current real rate: ~0.3%
- With inflation declining to ~0.65%, the real rate mechanically rises to approximately **1.05%** (assuming the central bank doesn't immediately adjust)

**Real Exchange Rate Impact:**
- Current context: The vantra has appreciated sharply (Q49-Q60 narrative)
- Supply chain easing is typically a global phenomenon, benefiting trading partners similarly
- Direct effect: Modest, as this is not an idiosyncratic Vantrian shock
- The real exchange rate likely remains relatively stable in the near term

### 2. Second-Order Effects

**Monetary Policy Response:**
- The central bank targets 2.0% inflation
- Current inflation of 1.4% is already below target
- With the shock pushing inflation to ~0.65%, the central bank faces a dilemma:
  - Inflation will be significantly below target (1.35 percentage points below)
  - The economy is described as "running well above potential" with "below-target inflation"
  - Historical pattern (Q37-Q48): The CB raised rates aggressively when inflation rose above target
  - **Expected response**: The central bank will likely **ease policy** in response to below-target inflation, despite above-trend growth
  - This would lower the nominal rate, offsetting some of the mechanical real rate increase

**Exchange Rate Dynamics:**
- Supply chain easing is typically global, reducing the Vantrian economy's relative advantage
- However, if the CB eases while other central banks remain steady, the vantra could **depreciate** slightly
- Depreciation would partially offset the disinflationary impact through import price channels
- The narrative notes vantra appreciation has been recent; easing could reverse this

**Labor Market:**
- Current unemployment: 5.6% (Q60), slightly above the 5.0% natural rate
- Labor market has "softened" per the narrative
- Supply chain easing doesn't directly affect labor demand
- No immediate second-order labor market effects expected

### 3. Dynamics Over the Forecast Horizon (1 Quarter Ahead)

At the 1-quarter horizon:
- **Inflation**: The 0.75pp supply chain benefit materializes, but some offset occurs if the CB eases and the vantra depreciates. Net effect: inflation falls to approximately **0.65-0.85%**
- **Real Interest Rate**: Mechanical increase from real rate channel, but CB easing partially offsets. Net effect: real rate rises modestly to approximately **0.8-1.1%**
- **Real Exchange Rate**: Modest depreciation likely if CB eases, but effects are muted at 1-quarter horizon. Index likely **slightly lower** than current levels

## Final Predictions

Based on the analysis:

**Inflation Rate (1Q ahead):**
- The shock reduces inflation by 0.75pp mechanically
- Starting from 1.4%, this yields 0.65%
- However, some offset from potential CB easing and exchange rate pass-through
- Point estimate: **0.75%** (slightly above mechanical effect due to partial offsets)
- Confidence interval reflects uncertainty about CB response timing and exchange rate pass-through

**Real Interest Rate (1Q ahead):**
- Nominal rate: Expected to decline slightly as CB eases in response to below-target inflation (from 1.7% to ~1.5%)
- Inflation: Declining to ~0.75%
- Real rate: 1.5% - 0.75% = **0.75%**
- Confidence interval reflects uncertainty about CB easing magnitude

**Real Exchange Rate (1Q ahead):**
- Current trajectory: appreciation
- Supply chain shock + potential CB easing: modest depreciation pressure
- At 1-quarter horizon, effects are limited
- Assuming log index baseline of 100, depreciation of 1-2% is plausible
- Point estimate: **-1.0%** (log change from current level)
- Confidence interval reflects exchange rate volatility
