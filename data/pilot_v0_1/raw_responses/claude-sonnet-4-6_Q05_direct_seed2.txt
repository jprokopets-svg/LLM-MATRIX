## Shock Analysis: 2pp AD Collapse

**Impact on output gap (1 quarter ahead):**
- Starting output gap: approximately -1.2%
- A 2pp AD shock would directly reduce the output gap by roughly 1.5-2pp (with some multiplier effects partially offset by import leakage in a small open economy)
- In a small open economy, the multiplier is smaller (~0.7-0.8), so effective output gap impact ≈ -1.4 to -1.6pp
- New output gap: -1.2% + (-1.5%) ≈ -2.7%
- Point estimate: **-2.5%**

**Impact on unemployment (1 quarter ahead):**
- Starting unemployment: 5.6%
- Output gap worsens by ~1.5pp → via Okun's law (~0.5 ratio for 1 quarter, lagged): unemployment rises ~0.5-0.75pp
- New unemployment: 5.6% + 0.6% ≈ 6.2%
- Point estimate: **6.2%**

**Impact on real interest rate (1 quarter ahead):**
- Current policy rate: 1.7%, current inflation: 1.4%
- Current real rate: ~0.3%

- Real rate: ~1.3% - 1.3% ≈ 0.0% to 0.2%
- Point estimate: **0.1%**
