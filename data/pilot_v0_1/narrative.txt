# Economic History of the Republic of Vantria

## Overview

The following is a quarterly economic review of the Republic of Vantria, a small open economy with a floating exchange rate regime. The central bank targets consumer price inflation at 2.0% annually and adjusts its policy rate accordingly. The domestic currency, the vantra (VTR), trades freely on international markets. The natural rate of unemployment is estimated at 5.0%. This report covers 60 quarters of economic history.
