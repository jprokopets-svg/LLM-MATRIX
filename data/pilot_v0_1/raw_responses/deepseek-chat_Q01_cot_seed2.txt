Let’s walk through the shock systematically.

**1. Direct effects of a 100 bp surprise rate hike**  
- The higher policy rate raises real interest rates immediately.  
- Consumption and investment fall, reducing aggregate demand.  
- Output gap turns negative (or becomes more negative if already negative).  
- With a lag, inflation declines as demand weakens.  
- Unemployment rises as firms cut back hiring or lay off workers.

**2. Second-order effects**  
- *Exchange rate channel*: Higher interest rates attract capital inflows → the vantra appreciates. This lowers import prices, adding further disinflationary pressure.  
- *Expectations channel*: If markets see the move as a credible commitment to low inflation, inflation expectations may decline, reinforcing the disinflation.  
- *Labor market*: As output falls below potential, the unemployment rate rises above the natural rate (5.0%). The tightening typically peaks within 2–4 quarters.

**3. Dynamics over 1 quarter horizon**  
- At 1 quarter ahead, the full effect on output is still building, but the immediate impact is significant.  
- Inflation responds with a lag, but the exchange rate appreciation can reduce import prices quickly, so some disinflation is visible.  
- Unemployment begins to rise as output slows.

**Baseline before shock (Q60)**:  
- Inflation = 1.4%  
- Unemployment = 5.6%  
- Output gap: The description says “economy running well above potential” yet disinflationary pressures and softening labor market suggest the gap was already closing. Given unemployment above natural rate (5.6% vs 5.0%), output gap was likely slightly negative (around –0.3% to –0.5%).

After 100 bp hike, output gap falls further. Typical short-run multiplier suggests output gap drops by ~0.5–0.8% for a 1% rate hike within one quarter. Starting from near zero, that gives output gap ≈ –0.8%.  

Inflation drops from 1.4% toward 1.0% or slightly below, given the appreciation effect.

Unemployment rises from 5.6% toward ~5.9% (Okun’s law: ~0.4% rise in unemployment per 1% output gap drop).
